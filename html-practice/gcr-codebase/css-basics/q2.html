Welcome to Jaipur

Jaipur, also known as the Pink City, is one of the most beautiful cities in India. It is famous for its forts, palaces, culture and traditional food.

History of Jaipur

Jaipur was founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II. The city was carefully planned according to traditional Indian architectural principles. Jaipur is famous for its beautiful buildings, wide streets and historic monuments.

The city has a rich royal history and attracts visitors from all over the world.

Famous Places

Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal is one of Jaipur's most famous landmarks. It is known for its unique windows and beautiful architecture.

Amber Fort

Amber Fort is a magnificent historical fort located on a hill. It is famous for its beautiful palaces and impressive architecture.

City Palace

City Palace is a large palace complex located in the heart of Jaipur. It represents the city's royal heritage.

Culture of Jaipur

Jaipur has a colorful and vibrant culture. Traditional music, dance, handicrafts, festivals and delicious food are important parts of the city's identity.

Visitors can enjoy traditional markets and experience the unique lifestyle of Rajasthan.
